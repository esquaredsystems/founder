Caregivers bringing children for routine immunisation have a predictable set of
questions — schedules, side effects, what to do after a missed dose — and often
no easy way to ask them. Bablibot answered those questions over SMS and
WhatsApp in Roman Urdu.

A feasibility study of this work was published in the *International Journal of
Medical Informatics* — see [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).
