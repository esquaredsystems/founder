I have been writing at [Vision 360](https://owaisahussain.blogspot.com/) since
long before it was fashionable to have opinions about batteries, and I keep a
separate Urdu blog for poetry. Neither of those is a good place to keep a
publication list or a record of the systems I have built. So: this site.

## What lives here

- **[Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})** — every system I have shipped since 2006, with a page each.
- **[Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }})** — peer-reviewed publications, with links to Scholar, Scopus and ResearchGate.
- **[Teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }})** — course outlines for everything I teach at KIET.
- **[About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }})** — the career history, education and skills.
- **Writing** — this section, for notes that belong next to the work rather than on a general-interest blog.

## What stays where it is

Vision 360 keeps its archive and its general technology writing. The Urdu blog
stays entirely separate. Both are linked from
[Writing]({{ '/writing/' | relative_url }}).

## How this is built

Jekyll, hosted on GitHub Pages, no plugins, no build step of my own. Adding a
post means adding a markdown file and pushing a commit. That is the whole
maintenance story, which is the point — a personal site that is annoying to
update stops getting updated.
