--- title: "You were on a mission." date: 2014-12-16 permalink: "/writing/2014/december/you-were-on-a-mission/" category: "poetry" description: "You were on a mission. To kill a ghost.." source_note: "(16-12-2014. In the memory of martyrs of Peshawar attack)" ---

You were on a mission.
To kill a ghost..
Your worst fear, which you wanted dead most..

He was on a mission too.
To catch butterflies..
And to tell his teacher, about the homework, some lies..

You couldn't wait.
Even for a second..
For seventy virgins were waiting for you in heaven..

Him! He already was in heaven.
He had all the time..
Didn't you hear him sing his favourite rhyme?

He's thinking.
Of her mother's goodbye kiss..
And how much him his butterflies will miss..

You aim.
You shoot.
To kill his smile..
He falls dead, yet he looks so alive..

You hail Takbeer.
But feel your soul burn..
That's when you know, his smile was stronger than your gun...
