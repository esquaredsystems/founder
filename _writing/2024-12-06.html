Disclaimer: This story is based on personal experience. Ahmed and Billu are fictional characters. Any resemblance with actual people will be purely accidental.

Ahmed and Billu are both freelance software developers, providing services on the same technologies. Ahmed charges $25/hour while Billu is happy with $10/hour. Thus, Ahmed often finds himself in a situation where he has to provide justification of why he deserves $25, where there’s already an abundance of likes of Billu who’d do the same job for half the price. He often has no clear answer to this question. At least not one that can be easily understood by many of his clients. On the surface, Ahmed and Billu are doing the same work, but differently in many ways:

AhmedBillu
Uses a high-end MacBook Pro for the development work. This computer compiles and runs a Rails web app in 10 seconds.Uses an El-cheapo Dell Vostro that compiles the same web app in 20 seconds.
Learned to type properly and types 50wpm (words per minute) utilizing all fingers and two thumbs.First, doesn’t have a good keyboard. Secondly, cannot type without looking at the keyboard. He is stuck at 25wpm.
Has subscribed to a dedicated 100Mbps Fiber connection from a reliable vendor. He can download, upload, stream, push and pull code within minutes.Is on a shared network, where speed hovers from 10 to 20 Mbps, depending on load and luck. He operates much slowly when working online.
Legally procures the software he uses, and ensures they are all up to date with latest suite of tools.Cracks everything, from MS Office to Photoshop, thus allowing various malware to live in his computer rent free. He has no idea of who is stealing his client’s precious data and code.
Never queries an entire database when working. He is laser focused on saving time. The average time a query takes him is 30 seconds.Loves “select * from massivetable” because it is faster to write. On his already slow computer and sluggish internet, an average query gives him results in 2-3 minutes.
Follows clean-code principles he learned during his degree programs and certifications. His projects are less prone to bugs and easy to make changes to. His pace of delivering work is like:
Alpha release in 20 days
Beta release in 10 days
V1 in 7 days
V2 in 7 days
…		Got himself trained on “mastering Python in 7 hours” kind of courses on YouTube. He produces buggy code, which take significantly more time to change afterwards. His pace of delivery is like:
Alpha release in 10 days
Beta in 20 days
V1 in 15 days
V2 in 15 days
…
Likes to play FPS games in his free time. Unconsciously, from his hand-eye coordination to the movement of his mouse, he is generally faster than average users. Additionally, he is focused on one task at a time.Consumes a ton of media. He always has a video or music running in background when working.

Neither Ahmed, nor Billu is being dishonest to their clients. What Billu does is not intentional; it’s simply the lack of experience and guidance for him that costs his clients.
