--- title: "Learn to let go" date: 2024-04-18 permalink: "/writing/2024/april/learn-to-let-go/" category: "misc" description: "Seven years, a dozen languages, countless tools — and one parting lesson from a mouse." ---

It would seem unusual when someone who rarely writes on LinkedIn chooses to post a picture of an ordinary mouse. Why would I write an ode to a mouse? Here’s a story of an unlikely teacher.

This A4Tech mouse, with a handy double click button was allocated to me some seven years ago (yes, seven) in my previous company and I loved it. It has been with me since then. Helping me, troubleshooting hundreds of problems on about a dozen languages, tens of projects and countless tools, defeating toughest of bosses in AAA games, choosing the best products from an ocean of online goods, sticking notes on research papers for my thesis, and generally surfing the web.

Today, it gave up on me. It didn’t just die all of a sudden. First the middle click, then right click, then the optical sensor started to miss now and then. Finally it was rendered useless. It. Broke.

I almost couldn’t believe this had happened, that a companion this loyal had gone.

But even on its last day, it gave me a parting gift. A valuable lesson. That I have to learn to let go... Let broken toys go; let obsolete ideas go; let hazardous habits go; let toxic people go. Why? Because room is to be made for a funnier toy, a newer idea, a healthier habit, a better person. Why? Because I also have to go. Someday. At once, or after slowly deteriorating. Why? Because in our ever changing world, nothing is meant to last forever. Why? Because this is precisely what makes it beautiful.

As I post this picture of an ordinary mouse, I smile at the extraordinary journey we shared and the wisdom it left behind.
