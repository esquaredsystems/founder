--- title: "9 years, 3 letters." date: 2022-12-23 permalink: "/writing/2022/december/nine-years-three-letters/" category: "misc" description: "A road to Pir Chanasi, a road to a doctorate, and the same question at the end of both — was it worth it?" ---

“Beautiful!”, “Awe-inspiring!”, “Breathtaking!”? How would you define this picture? How about “worthy!”? Seems like a world out of place, right? For an ordinary spectator, yes. But the word “worthy” is closest to what I felt when I took that picture 3 years ago. Not because it described what the eyes were beholding, but because what the heart felt. I had been traveling for 3 days. From Karachi to Kashmir. It’s a 1600km journey, horizontally, and 3km, vertically. And at the end of the road, standing on the top of Pir Chanasi, glancing at the path I travelled with my friends, I had to ask myself: “was it worth it?”. I’d have been sorely disappointed had the answer been anything but “worthy”. And this is where that journey was concluded.

Just like the journey to my doctorate concluded yesterday, when the esteemed members of defense committee congratulated me on completion of my PhD. It took nine years of grit, patience, and painstaking research to be able to write those 3 letters with my name. But was it worth it? Let’s rewind the clock to yesterday to seek the answer together.

December 22nd, 12:50pm. I’m wrapping up to leave the conference room in College of Computing and Information Sciences. Unplugging wires. Shutting down the laptop. The IT staff assisting during the presentation warmly congratulate me. I’m now walking out, heading to my supervisor’s room. He is greeting me with smile: “Congratulations! Dr. Owais”. We’re chewing some facts. Sharing jokes. Oh! Wait. There’s a bunch of documents I need to sign and get signed. I’m now walking door to door.

Whoever I meet is welcoming with the same warm smile. Wait! The dean just shook my hands. TWICE! And more of the same from faculty to administrative staff. An hour has past but I’m done here. I’m now walking to the parking. My phone is constantly buzzing. WhatsApp never wastes a second. But wait! Why am I not feeling like dancing? No tears of joy? Not even a punch in the air? Huh! A mere sigh, breath-in breath-out? I need to investigate why I’m not excited. And the answer is there, it always was. I better not forget the answer.

PhD is not exactly something to be too excited about, Owais. You had your contribution, and you can take some pride. But you should realize that in the vast ocean of knowledge, your input is minuscule, a mere bubble that a random wave casted, which will pop as the next wave comes. If anything, you came to know how very little you know about life, universe, and everything. But that’s a fact you know, those around you will now see you differently. Your shoulders were relieved from the weight you’ve been carrying for the past decade, only to now lift a heavier responsibility. You have a say, and people will listen to you, trusting that you are a legitimate source. Not only in your own domain of expertise, but in anything you say. The society assumes that a person of higher intellectual stature will not blemish his tongue by speaking of what he has not validated himself. So, be cautious with your words and advice. The weight of this new responsibility will forever be on your shoulders.

But what about the original question? Worthy!? Surely, for now, hopefully. I guess for a concrete, definitive answer, that question will have to wait. Wait, till I’m drawing my last breath. I wish and pray to almighty that the answer is not a disappointing one.
